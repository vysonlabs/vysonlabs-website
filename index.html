Products

Current product work

We are building focused digital products with clear use cases, approachable design, and responsible product foundations.

LN

LumaNest

In development

LumaNest is an educational app being designed for children aged 4-8. It aims to help young learners explore subjects such as animals, nature, space, mathematics, English, puzzles, and general knowledge through age-appropriate visual activities.

  • Visual learning experiences
  • Flashcards and quizzes
  • Start, Grow, and Shine learning levels
  • Child-friendly navigation and parent-conscious design

Intended audience: Families with young learners aged 4-8.

LO

Lumora

Concept

Lumora is a personal finance and organisation product currently in concept development. It is intended to help users understand and manage everyday financial information through a clear and approachable experience.

  • Everyday financial organisation
  • Clear information structure
  • Approachable mobile experience
  • Designed for practical personal decision-making

Intended audience: Everyday users looking for simple personal organisation tools.
