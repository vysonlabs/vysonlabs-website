LN

LumaNest

In development

LumaNest is an educational app being designed for children aged 4-8. It aims to help young learners explore subjects such as animals, nature, space, mathematics, English, puzzles, and general knowledge through age-appropriate visual activities.

  • Visual learning experiences
  • Flashcards and quizzes
  • Start, Grow, and Shine learning levels
  • Child-friendly navigation and parent-conscious design

Intended audience: Families with young learners aged 4-8.

LO

Lumora

In development

Lumora is a private personal finance vault currently in development. It is being designed to help users record, organise, value, and project their assets, liabilities, insurance cover, and important ownership documents without relying on app-owned storage for personal financial records.

  • Local-first asset and liability records
  • Encrypted document vault for policies, loans, and ownership files
  • Net worth, insurance, and future projection views
  • Designed around user-controlled data and optional personal cloud backup

Intended audience: Individuals who want a clear, private view of their long-term financial position.
